# PCOB weapon IDs

The lookup table for `curWeaponID` (`gettotalplayerlist`) and `WeaponID`
(`getplayerweapondetailinfo`).

Retrieved 2026-08-17 from the Google Drive link printed beside `curWeaponID` in
`specs/new/[updated] PC OB API List.pdf` (file name `Gunlist-ID.xlsx`, 56 rows, one sheet). The
English column is as the publisher wrote it — note that `102002` is labelled with a skin name
("Dragonfire - UMP45") rather than the base weapon, and that `103901` / `103903` duplicate the
Kar98K and AWM under separate ids.

Not needed for the leaderboard overlay. Kept because a kill feed or a post-match weapon breakdown
would need it, and the link may not stay reachable.
